# Privacy Policy — StoreFlow POS

Effective date: 3 October 2026

StoreFlow POS ("the app") is a point-of-sale application for Windows
published by XWS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with
information.

## Summary

- The app works **offline**. It has no online account, no cloud server and no
  analytics, advertising or tracking.
- Everything you enter (products, prices, sales, customers, users, settings,
  photos) is stored **only on your computer**.
- **We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.**

## Information stored on your device

To run your shop, the app stores the information you enter in a local
database on your computer, including:

- business and store details, products, prices, stock and suppliers;
- sales, payments, refunds, cash drawer entries and reports;
- customer records you choose to create (for example name, phone, email,
  address, loyalty points and credit balances);
- user accounts for your staff. Passwords are stored only as salted
  PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes, never in readable form;
- product images and a receipt logo you choose, copied into the app's data
  folder;
- appearance and printer preferences.

You control this information. You can edit or deactivate records in the app,
and you can delete all data by uninstalling the app (Microsoft Store version)
or deleting the app data folder.

## Camera

If you use camera barcode scanning, the app uses your webcam only while the
scanner window is open, to read barcodes. Images are processed on your
computer and are not saved or transmitted.

## Files you choose

When you import or export CSV files, save PDFs, choose a logo or product
photo, or create and restore backups, the app reads or writes only the files
and folders you select.

## Network use

The app does not connect to the internet. Links to email or WhatsApp in
*Help & support* open your own email app or browser, which are governed by
their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

## Your customers' data

If you record personal data about your customers, you are responsible for
handling it according to the laws that apply to your business (for example,
telling customers why you keep their phone number and deleting it on
request).

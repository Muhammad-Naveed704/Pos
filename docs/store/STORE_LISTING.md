Fast, offline point of sale for shops, pharmacies, groceries and retail

## Description

StoreFlow POS is a complete point-of-sale and back-office app for Windows.
Everything runs on your computer, so your counter keeps working even when the
internet is down, and your business data stays private.

SELL FASTER
• Scan with a USB barcode scanner or your webcam, or tap product cards with
  photos
• Keyboard shortcuts for every step (F2 search, F7 pay, F8 exact cash)
• Split payments (cash, card, bank/wallet), change calculation, discounts,
  held sales and notes
• Credit and partial-payment sales with due dates and credit limits
• Returns, refunds and voids with stock and cash handled automatically
• Print to 58/80 mm thermal printers or A4/A5 invoices, or save PDF

MANAGE YOUR SHOP
• Products with photos, barcodes, categories and sub-categories, cost and
  profit
• Bulk import and export with CSV (Excel)
• Multi-store stock, adjustments, stock counts, transfers and full movement
  history
• Purchases from suppliers and supplier returns
• Cash drawer sessions with pay-ins, pay-outs and over/short

CUSTOMERS & CREDIT
• Customer profiles, loyalty points and purchase history
• Customer ledger, statements (PDF/print), payments and overdue tracking
• Receivables dashboard

INSIGHTS
• Live dashboard: sales, invoices, cash, discounts, top products, payment
  methods, recent sales and inventory health
• Reports for sales, profit, categories, cashiers, payments, purchases,
  stock value and receivables; export CSV or PDF

SAFE AND SECURE
• Users and roles with detailed permissions
• Owner recovery key for forgotten passwords
• One-file backups with checksum verification, automatic scheduled backups
  and safe restore

Works offline. No account. No subscription. No ads. No tracking.

## Notes for certification (Partner Center → Submission options)

StoreFlow POS is an offline desktop app. To test: complete the first-run
setup (any business name; create an owner username and a password of 8+
characters; note the recovery key), then add a product and make a sale.
No account, server or network access is required.
The *webcam* capability is used only for optional barcode scanning in the
POS and product editor. The app uses *runFullTrust* because it is a desktop
(Win32) Flutter app that needs a local SQLite database, printing through the
Windows print spooler and user-chosen file access for CSV, PDF and backups.
